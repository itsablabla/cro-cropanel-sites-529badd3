CRO Page Prioritization

Where the Conversion
Leaks Are

The most valuable conversion mechanism on this site is the coverage-check form, which is the primary entry point for users to determine if the service is available at their address. This mechanism is bleeding value because the homepage presents multiple competing CTAs that dilute focus, and the /pricing page fails to render, breaking the path for users seeking plan details. Additionally, the lack of clear plan context and security trust markers around the form increases friction and reduces trust, causing users to abandon before completing the coverage check.
